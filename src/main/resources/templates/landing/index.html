About CIVICSHIELD

A Smarter Way to Fix Infrastructure

CIVICSHIELD connects citizens directly with government departments. Our system automatically checks for duplicates, calculates risk levels and assigns reports to the right team.

Administrators track every report, assign workers, monitor deadlines and ensure high-priority problems get attention first.
